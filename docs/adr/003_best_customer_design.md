# ADR-002 : Best Customer using RFM style scoring

## Status
Accepted

## Context
Stakeholder asks for their 'best customer' to keep track on how they do and what's decision could be made based on this data.

## Decision
We will choose RFM style scoring system with how recent they placed an order, how frequent they made a purchase, and how much money do they spent in general.

## Alternative Considerations
- other scoring system - Rejected. due to the limitation of the project timeline, we need to keep it doable for the first version.

## Consequences
The scoring system is simpler and more basic, reducing the detail of accuracy but still meeting the needs of the stakeholder.
